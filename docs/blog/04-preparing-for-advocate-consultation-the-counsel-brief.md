# How to Consult an Advocate Effectively: Turning Contract Confusion into an Actionable Brief

*By CounterDraft Legal Research Group*

---

### The Anatomy of an Inefficient Legal Consultation

Most individuals and business founders approach legal consultations with an advocate in one of two suboptimal ways:

1. **The Overwhelmed Handover**: The client prints a 45-page Master Services Agreement or commercial lease, drops it on the advocate's desk, and asks: *"Is this safe for me to sign?"*  
   *Result*: The advocate must spend several billable hours reading routine boilerplate provisions (definitions, notice addresses, severability) before finding the three clauses that actually threaten the client's interests.
2. **The Vague Verbal Summary**: The client recalls a few points from memory: *"I think there was something about a non-compete, and maybe a deposit penalty, but I'm not sure of the exact wording."*  
   *Result*: The advocate can only offer generic, non-committal legal truisms without being able to assess the precise enforceability of the specific text.

---

### The Power of the Structured Consultation Memorandum

In high-stakes corporate law, senior partners do not read entire agreements from scratch during preliminary strategy meetings. Instead, associates prepare a **Counsel Briefing Memorandum**: a concise, 1-to-2 page executive summary that isolates:
- Core commercial context and executing parties.
- Top statutory vulnerabilities ranked by severity.
- Exact clause text accompanied by specific statutory questions.
- Critical missing safeguards (force majeure, cure periods, escrow protections).

By preparing a structured brief before stepping into an advocate's office, prospective signatories immediately elevate the quality of their legal counsel while dramatically reducing consultation overhead.

---

### Key Questions to Direct Toward Your Advocate

When reviewing flagged contract clauses, avoid asking broad questions like *"Is this fair?"* Instead, ask targeted, jurisdiction-specific questions:

1. **On Unilateral Termination**:
   > *"Given the lack of a cure period in Section 6.2, if the counterparty issues a notice of termination, what are my interim injunctive remedies in our local High Court, and what is the typical timeline to obtain an ad-interim stay?"*
2. **On Liquidated Damages & Forfeiture**:
   > *"Under Section 74 of the Indian Contract Act and the Kailash Nath ruling, if we refuse to accept the forfeiture of our security deposit, what is our most cost-effective recovery path: a summary suit under Order XXXVII of the CPC, or mediation?"*
3. **On Asymmetric Liability Caps**:
   > *"If the counterparty insists on keeping their liability capped at one month of subscription fees, can we insert an express carve-out for data breach notification expenses and regulatory penalties under the DPDPA 2023?"*
4. **On Territorial Jurisdiction & Dispute Resolution**:
   > *"The dispute clause mandates exclusive jurisdiction in Mumbai, whereas the leased property and our operations are in Bengaluru. How enforceable is this forum selection clause under Section 20 of the Code of Civil Procedure?"*
